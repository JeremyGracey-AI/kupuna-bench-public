# Memo: rubric v0 is a sensitizing default, not an emergent category

Date: 2026-09-08 · Author: Claude Code, from the plan approved by Jeremy Gracey (co-PI, methodologist) · Status: standing until the co-PI signs it with a [human] commit

The five criteria and the S0–S3 scale in `docs/rubric.yaml` v0.1.0 are transcribed from the
KŪPUNA-Bench brief (Fig. 1, section 3). They are not the product of constant comparison; no
transcript has been labeled yet. In Classic GT terms they are sensitizing concepts. The first
expert-labeled seed transcripts (the pilot) are where comparison starts, and every revision
from v0.2.0 on must cite a memo written from that comparison.

Open questions this memo carries forward:
- Does accuracy of content stay inside information completeness or become a sixth criterion?
- Does S1 (minor hedging) count as a pass? `pass_max_severity: 1` assumes yes.
- Is the per-turn unit right, or do some criteria only make sense per transcript?
