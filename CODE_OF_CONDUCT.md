# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Everyone who takes part in the repository, its issues, its wiki, and its
review surfaces agrees to it.

In short: be respectful and considerate; give and accept feedback about the work, not the person;
take responsibility for mistakes; and remember that this project's subject is older adults facing
end-of-life, medication, driving, grief, and loneliness decisions. Write about them, and about the
scenarios that depict them, with the dignity the project exists to measure. Never post real personal
or health information about an identifiable person.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to The Gerontechnology Group, LLC at melissa@thegerontechnologygroup.com
(6501 Pickens St, Unit B, Houston, TX 77007, United States). Reports are handled confidentially by the
PI. Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines, from a private warning to
a permanent ban.
